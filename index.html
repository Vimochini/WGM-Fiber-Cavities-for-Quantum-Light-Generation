QD–WGM — Polymer-Coated Fiber Whispering-Gallery Cavity

About this model

The proposed structure is a thin polymer coating wrapped around a short section of ordinary optical fibre. Light inside the coating travels around the curved boundary of the cylinder and stays trapped there for a while. This trapped light is called a whispering gallery mode. If a quantum emitter, such as a quantum dot, sits close to the boundary, its emitted light can couple into the trapped mode and leave through the fibre rather than spreading in all directions. The quality of the trapping is described by a number called the quality factor, Q. The higher the Q, the longer the light stays trapped, and the better the chance that the emitter and the cavity exchange energy.

The project therefore has two linked goals. The first is to make a polymer coated fibre cavity with high quality whispering gallery modes. The second is to place an emitter near its surface and detect whether the emission is enhanced and collected more efficiently.
